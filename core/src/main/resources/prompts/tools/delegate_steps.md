Hand a short, concrete local goal on the screen you are using now to a fast step-picker, which reads the screen, picks one listed action at a time (tap, scroll, back, or typing one of your texts) and repeats up to maxSteps actions (default and maximum {{maxSteps}}) until it reaches the goal or gets stuck. Use it for plain navigation and finding on the current app or page: open a known tab, menu or settings entry, scroll to and open a named item, type your exact search text into the search box and run the search. Do not use it when the next move needs judgement (which item or option is right, whether something is correct), when you need to read, compare or summarize content, or for sending, posting, paying, ordering, deleting or submitting: it never does those and hands back before them. Write the goal as the end state you want to see, naming exact labels from the screen when you know them. Give in texts every exact string it may type; it types nothing else. It hands back with status done, blocked or stopped and a reason, the actions it really ran (with whether the screen changed), and the current screen. It does not judge success for you: check that screen before you go on.
